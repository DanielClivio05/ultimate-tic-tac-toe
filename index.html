Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

Rules
  1. X goes first and can play anywhere.
  2. After a move, your opponent must play on the same small board.
  3. Three in a row on a small board wins that board.
  4. If you are sent to a board that is won or full, you can play in any open board.
  5. Win three small boards in a row to win the game.
